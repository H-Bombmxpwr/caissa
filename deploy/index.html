Caissa

A desktop chess workbench — a lighter ChessBase.

Your games stay on your own disk as ordinary PGN. A bundled Stockfish analyses them, the explorer walks your openings and shows how they actually went, and your repertoire drills itself. No account, no cloud library, no telemetry.

Download

Also for Windows, macOS and Linux · run from source

A database you own

PGN files in folders you can read and back up. The index is only an index.

Openings, judged

Walk a collection's tree and see the score, the trend, and where the points went.

Repertoires that drill

A lichess study becomes spaced-repetition lines you play blindfold.

Works offline

The engine, your games, the opening book and every exercise need no connection.
